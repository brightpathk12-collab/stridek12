The Framework

Six Domains of AI Literacy

STRIDE organizes AI literacy into six interconnected domains, threaded with C3 meta-competencies: Critical Thinking, Creativity, and Collective Judgment.

S

Sense

Understand how AI works — from pattern recognition to neural architectures. Students see AI running locally and observe how input quality affects output.

T

Think

Critically evaluate AI outputs: detect bias, fact-check responses, question sources. The iterative revision loop makes critical thinking the default behavior.

R

Relate

Examine AI's real-world impact on people, communities, equity, and power. A privacy-first architecture is itself a lesson in data ethics.

I

Innovate

Create responsibly with AI through structured prompt engineering, attribution, and ethical use. Seven specialized AI agents guide responsible creation.

D

Decide

Know when to use AI and when not to. Students earn AI access through quality thinking — the traffic light model ensures they demonstrate readiness first.

E

Empower

Build student agency: lead AI conversations, mentor peers, advocate for equity. Analytics make growth visible from guided play to responsible innovation.

STRIDE + LIA2

Not Just a Framework — A Working System

STRIDE defines what students need to learn. LIA2, our locally-hosted AI platform, defines how they practice it. Together, they form something no other K-12 product offers.

The PROMPT Thinking Cycle

P

Problem

Define the task

R

Research

Show your work

O

Objective

Set a goal

M

Methods

Set constraints

P

Prototype

Write the prompt

T

Tune

Evaluate & iterate

Every student interaction follows a structured 6-step protocol: Problem, Research/Record, Objective, Methods/Modifiers, Prototype Prompt, and Tune. Scored on a 20-point rubric, this framework transforms vague AI requests into rigorous thinking exercises.

Students don't just use AI — they earn the right to AI assistance by demonstrating structured thinking first.

Traffic Light Model

Red — Clarify

Prompt is vague or shows no prior effort. The system asks clarifying questions only — no hints, no agent access. Students must articulate what they need.

Yellow — Guide

Some work shown but prompt is incomplete. The system provides hints, guiding questions, and partial scaffolding. No complete answers.

Green — Collaborate

PROMPT steps completed, score 15/20 or above. Full agent access with detailed support. Students have earned autonomous AI collaboration.
